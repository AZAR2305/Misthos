# For buyers

You owe money and may have cash to spare.

## Get set up

Open **Overview**, connect your wallet and choose **Buyer**. Your **Treasury** shows the USDC in your wallet, what you may still spend today and the approved invoices you could pay early.

## Approve invoices

On **Approvals**, review each invoice. Approving says the work is done and starts the clock. You can also **dispute** an invoice, which blocks any early payment.

## Pay early

When the supplier's offer is acceptable you allow the contract to move the USDC, then pay. You see the net amount, the discount, the effective yearly rate and the fee before you confirm. The discount is yours to keep.

## Your limits

On **Policy** you set a per-invoice cap and a daily cap on-chain. The contract refuses any payment above them, whatever any agent says. Settings such as your hurdle rate, your cash floor and the amount you sign off personally stay with your own agent and are never sent to the supplier.

## Your cash is protected

The allocator never plans a payment that would take you under your cash floor or leave payroll and bills uncovered.
