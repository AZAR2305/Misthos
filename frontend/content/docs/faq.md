# FAQ

**Is the price always a discount?** No. The first rung is 0%, and when the buyer has idle cash it is often accepted right there.

**Who pays the fee?** The buyer pays a small success fee on the discount. The supplier never pays one.

**Can an AI move my money?** No. A model can propose; plain code checks; the contract decides. Nothing a model writes reaches a signer.

**What if I do nothing?** The invoice is paid on its due date as usual. Early payment is optional on both sides.

**Do I need an account or an email?** No email, ever. Where the site has accounts turned on, you sign in by signing a message with your wallet. Every public page can be read without signing in.

**Is it safe to try?** It runs on a test network with free test USDC. The contract is unaudited, so do not use funds you cannot lose.

**Where does the data come from?** Straight from the chain. A number the site cannot read shows as "unavailable", never as a guess.
