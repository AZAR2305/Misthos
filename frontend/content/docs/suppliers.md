# For suppliers

You are owed money and want it sooner.

## Get set up

1. Open **Overview**, sign in if the site asks, connect your wallet and choose **Supplier**.
2. Make sure your wallet is on the right network (the header shows a warning if it is not).
3. You need a little USDC in the wallet to cover the network fee. See [Wallet and USDC](/docs/wallet-and-usdc).

## Create and sign an invoice

Go to **New invoice**. Enter the buyer's wallet address, the amount in USDC, the due date and a reference you will recognise. You sign it **once** with your wallet. The contract records it, and nobody can change it afterwards.

## Offers

After the buyer approves, your agent (or you, by hand) can offer early payment. Offers start at **0%** and climb one rung at a time, never above the limit set by what waiting costs you. You always see the price in **dollars** before anything is sent.

## Getting paid

When the buyer accepts a rung, the contract pays you **in USDC** straight away and closes the invoice. The page shows the exact amount you received, the discount and a link to the transaction.

## Good to know

- You can pause an invoice's offers at any time.
- The discount is a simple rate over the exact days you skip, rounded in your favour.
- The success fee is paid by the buyer, never by you.
