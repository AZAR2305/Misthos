Why it works

  • Zero is a valid price

    When a buyer has idle cash, being paid early can cost the supplier nothing. The supplier's agent opens at 0% and climbs only while waiting would cost more. Very often, the answer is zero.

  • Priced to the day, never padded

    The discount is a simple rate over the exact seconds you skip, rounded in the supplier's favour. What you see before you sign is what the contract pays.

  • The model proposes. Code decides.

    No AI output ever reaches a signer or a contract call. Caps, a 50% rate ceiling and single-use offers are enforced on Arc, so even a fooled agent can only do what the limits allow.

How it works

  1. 01

    Sign

    The supplier signs the invoice with a wallet. No gas and no browser extension.

  2. 02

    Register

    InvoiceBook on Arc records the signed invoice once. Duplicates are rejected.

  3. 03

    Approve

    The buyer accepts the work. Only approved invoices can be paid early.

  4. 04

    Offer

    The supplier agent posts a short ladder of signed offers, starting at 0%. The buyer agent accepts the first rung at or above its private hurdle.

  5. 05

    Pay early

    The contract pays the supplier in USDC and closes the invoice exactly once. A small success fee on the discount is paid by the buyer, never by the supplier.

  • Starts at 0% and climbs a short ladder of signed offers.
