Reading the chain…

What is this page?

Where the numbers come from. Your browser reads the contract's events straight from Local chain and does the arithmetic itself. There is no server in between and no typed-in figure. Every settlement links to the explorer so you can check it.

How we count. An invoice counts once it is paid early. "Days early" is the whole days between payment and the due date. The annual rate is the discount over the cash paid and the time skipped.

What this does not show yet. All activity on this network is counted together. Separating real pilot invoices from the team's own test runs needs the backend, which is not built, so treat these as testnet activity.

{testnet} All activity on Local chain, including test runs. Not a measure of customers.
