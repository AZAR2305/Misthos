The agents

Two independent programs, one for each business. They negotiate the price of waiting in signed messages and never see each other's private numbers.

What is this page?

Why two? Each side has secrets. The supplier knows what waiting costs it. The buyer knows its minimum rate and its cash plan. If one program held both, one side would have to trust the other. So each runs separately, with its own wallet, and they only exchange signed messages.

What an agent can and cannot do. An AI model may suggest where to start and what to flag, but it can never sign or send anything. Plain code checks every number, and the contract has the final say. If the model, the wallet or the network fails, the agent does nothing and asks a person.

How they talk

  1. 1The supplier agent signs an offer, starting at 0%, and sends it.signed
  2. 2The buyer agent checks the signature, the invoice and its own rules.code
  3. 3It either pays at that rate, or answers with a reason code and never the number behind it.signed
  4. 4If rejected, the supplier signs the next rung, a little higher, up to its own limit.≤ 6 rungs
The seven reason codes

BELOW_HURDLE The offered rate is under the buyer's private minimum. The minimum itself is never revealed.

LIQUIDITY_FLOOR Paying would take the buyer's cash under the floor it keeps.

NOT_ELIGIBLE The invoice is not approved, is already due, or the offer breaks a cap.

NEEDS_HUMAN_APPROVAL The payment is above the amount the buyer signs off personally, so a person decides.

EXPIRED The offer ran out of time before it was accepted.

DISPUTED The buyer disputed the invoice.

REPUTATION_FLAG A record of past behaviour blocks early payment.
